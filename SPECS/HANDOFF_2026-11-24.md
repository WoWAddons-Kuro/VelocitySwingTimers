## Traps that bit this session (don't repeat)

- **Lua forward references in `ShotTracker.lua`** — 4 times. A `local function` declared below a closure that calls
  it is a nil upvalue there. Rule: declare locals at the top of their section, or assign (`Name = function`) to a
  forward `local Name`. Check with `luac -l | Select-String 'GETGLOBAL.*; <ourLocal>'` (empty = clean). Planned fix:
  split §12.15/12.17 diagnostics into `Core\ShotDiagnostics.lua` (todo).
- **`pcall(RegisterUnitEvent)` with a pcall fallback hides failures** — now recorded in `NS.features["Event.*"]`.
- **Modelling from memory** — the "GCD blocks Auto Shot" model was wrong and research + the owner's logic killed it.
  `_EXTERNAL_APIS\wow_swing_timer_api.md` has the verified mechanics; read it before touching shot timing.
